Privacy Policy

A's Life · last updated 7 November 2026

A's Life is a personal planner app. One person built it for their own use. It is not a commercial service and it has no other users. This policy explains what the app does with data, including data from Pinterest.

What the app stores

The app stores the lists and notes you add yourself, such as plans, recipes, wardrobe pieces and social media posts. It keeps them in a private database (Supabase) that only the signed-in owner can read. The app has no ads and no analytics trackers, and it never sells or shares data.

Pinterest data

When you connect your Pinterest account, the app uses the official Pinterest API to:

  • read your profile name and your account statistics (impressions, saves and clicks);
  • read your own boards and the pins on the boards you choose, to show them in your moodboard, your inspiration list and as a daily affirmation;
  • create pins on your own boards, but only when you tap "Pin on Pinterest" yourself;
  • read the statistics of pins you created from the app.

The app only works with your own account. It never accesses other people's accounts and never posts anything without your action. Your Pinterest access token sits in the private database and is only used for the actions above.

Other connected services

You can also connect Instagram and TikTok to read your own posts and statistics. These follow the same rules: only your own account, only for your own overview, and never shared.

Keeping and deleting data

Your data stays until you delete it. You can delete any item in the app. You can disconnect Pinterest at any time under your Pinterest settings (pinterest.com/settings, under the apps you have given access). After that, the app can no longer reach your Pinterest account. Pins that were already copied to your moodboard stay until you delete them in the app.

Contact

For questions about this policy, open an issue on the project page on GitHub.
